# Bubble Sort, One Tiny Rule

How do computers sort millions of numbers?

Let's start simple: Bubble Sort.

Compare neighbours. Left one bigger? Swap.

If not, leave them.

The biggest gets carried to the end, and locks.

Repeat on what's left.

No swaps? It's sorted.

But there's one problem: Bubble Sort is terrible at sorting huge datasets.

A million numbers could take five hundred billion comparisons.
