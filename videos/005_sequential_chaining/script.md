# Sequential Chaining

Let's understand sequential chaining.

Imagine you're running a coffee shop and want AI to create an Instagram campaign for your new coffee.

You could have given ChatGPT one massive prompt asking it to understand your audience, come up with ideas, write captions, and review everything.

But what if you broke that task into smaller steps instead?

First, ask AI to identify your target audience and the coffee's key selling points.

Take that output and pass it to the next prompt to create a content outline.

Then use that outline to write an engaging caption.

Finally, send that caption for a quality check.

Did you notice something?

Every step builds on the result of the previous one.

This is called sequential chaining, breaking complex tasks into smaller prompts where the output of one becomes the input of the next.

It's easier to manage, debug, improve.

But be careful, a single mistake in an early step can affect the entire process.

That is sequential chaining for you.
