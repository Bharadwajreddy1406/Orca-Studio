# QR Codes

<!-- Square brackets are ElevenLabs v3 delivery tags. They are performed, not spoken, and never appear on screen. -->

[curious] Spilled coffee on the menu's QR code?

Smudged. Scratched. Torn.

Ask for a new menu? Ugh.

[mischievously] What if it just... scans anyway?

Your link becomes bits. Black and white squares.

Three corner squares mark the spot, and which way's up.

[excited] Then extra repair data, computed from your message.

Now, splash! A chunk is gone.

The scanner rebuilds the missing pieces.

[excited] Your link pops right back!

That's Reed-Solomon error correction.

Up to thirty percent can go missing.

Hide too much, or a corner square? No scan.

[excited] Happy scanning!
