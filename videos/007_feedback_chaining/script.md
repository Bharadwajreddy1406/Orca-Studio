# Feedback Chaining

<!-- Square brackets are ElevenLabs v3 delivery tags. They are performed, not spoken, and never appear on screen. -->

[curious] AI wrote your code... [sighs] and it's broken. Again.

Now you're stuck fixing it yourself.

[excited] But what if AI tested its own code?

The test runs.

It fails, and sends back the error.

AI reads that feedback... and fixes the code.

Then it tests again.

Fail. Fix. Test. Until it passes.

Or until it hits a stopping limit.

[excited] That's Feedback Chaining.

Every attempt learns from the last one.

Just remember... without a stopping limit, it can loop forever.

[laughs] Know when to stop!
