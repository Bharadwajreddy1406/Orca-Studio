# Iterative Refinement

<!-- Square brackets are ElevenLabs v3 delivery tags. They are performed, not spoken, and never appear on screen. -->

[sighs] Big meeting tomorrow... and your AI slides look meh.

Start over? No way.

[curious] What if you improved them, one pass at a time?

Pass one: fix the structure.

Pass two: simplify the words.

Pass three: sharper examples, polished slides.

Every version beats the last one.

[excited] That's Iterative Refinement.

Small, focused passes... until it's good enough.

Just remember... decide what good enough means, or you'll polish forever.

[excited] Go nail that meeting!
